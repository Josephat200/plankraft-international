The Practice

Who We Are

Plankraft International is a design practice founded on the conviction that architecture is one of the most consequential acts of human creativity. We are a lean, focused studio — built on talent, not scale — where each project receives the full weight of our collective expertise and personal investment.

We operate at the intersection of design rigour and practical delivery, bridging the conceptual and the constructed with equal fluency. Our team brings diverse strengths to every commission: from the precision of BIM workflows to the tactile craft of physical model-making, from the sweep of strategic planning to the discipline of on-site execution.

We believe the best architecture emerges from curiosity, collaboration, and an uncompromising commitment to craft — and we bring all three to every project we touch.

Our Architects
Mwita Christopher Mangiti
Architect

Mwita is an architect with a broad design range spanning commercial and hospitality projects, with a strong command of both architecture and interior design. He approaches every project holistically — from initial concept and spatial planning through to detailed design, documentation, and construction. His fluency in BIM workflows ensures precision and coordination at every stage of the process. On-site, he applies the same rigour to supervision, ensuring design intent is preserved through to completion. Skilled in 3D visualisation and miniature model-making, he brings ideas to life early, giving clients a clear and confident picture of the finished project. He takes each commission from brief to handover, managing the full journey with clarity and care.
